Hey! I've been building something for a while and want to finally share it — it's called Adiyan.

It's an AI agent harness that runs on your own machine, not someone else's cloud. A few things about it:

🔗 *Peer-to-peer* — if your machine's ever too busy, Adiyan can ask a peer's Adiyan for help, but only when you explicitly say yes for that one message. I haven't come across another AI agent that works this way.

💻 *Runs locally* — your assistant, its memory, all of it runs on your own hardware. Your conversations stay where you put them, not on a server somewhere else.

📚 *Built-in RAG* — upload your own documents and it actually searches and reasons over them, not just chats.

🛠️ *Built to extend* — if you're a developer, there's a dev kit: copy one folder, write one function, and your own agent is live and discoverable, no routing code needed.

And you just talk to it on WhatsApp — no new app to install.

Take a look: https://bharanitharanr.github.io/adiyan/

Would genuinely love to know what you think.
