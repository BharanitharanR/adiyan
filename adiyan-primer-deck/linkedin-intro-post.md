I've been building something for a while and want to finally share it — it's called Adiyan.

It's an AI agent harness that runs on your own machine. Think of the peer-to-peer piece like a torrent, but for shared LLM compute — sharing is caring. If your machine's ever too busy, Adiyan can ask a peer's Adiyan for help, but only when you explicitly say yes for that one message. I haven't come across another AI agent that works this way.

A few other things about it:

→ Runs locally — your assistant and its memory run on your own hardware. Your conversations stay where you put them, not on someone else's server.

→ Built-in RAG — upload your own documents and it actually searches and reasons over them, not just chats.

→ Adiyan Reader, out of the box — upload a PDF and tell it to read, and it reads you one page a day.

→ Built to extend — there's a dev kit for developers: copy one folder, write one function, and your own agent is live and discoverable. No routing code needed.

And you just talk to it on WhatsApp — no new app to install.

Take a look: https://bharanitharanr.github.io/adiyan/
