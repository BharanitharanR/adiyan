Where does Sarvam-1 actually sit in Adiyan right now?

Short answer first, then the diagram, then how it got there.

Nowhere in the live pipeline. Sarvam-1 was tried, tested with real evidence, wired in, and then removed - all in this session. It's still pulled locally in Ollama, but nothing in the running mesh calls it today.

Then vs. now

Toggle between the design that got built first, and what actually shipped.

Why it changed

Three real turns in this session, in order.

v1 built and proven, narrowly
Sarvam-1 (a raw completion model, not chat-tuned) was few-shot wrapped into a Question/Answer shape with a stop sequence, and confirmed live to correctly answer a real Tamil question - the actual test evidence held up. But it answered directly, with no access to search_knowledge_base, analyse_this, memory, or any other skill.
Real-world test exposed the actual gap
A real WhatsApp voice note (not a clean synthetic test sample) transcribed to garbage - Whisper mangled English technical terms mixed into natural Tamil speech. That test made the deeper problem visible: even a perfect transcription would only ever get a standalone sarvam-1 answer, never a real business answer grounded in your knowledge base.
Redesigned: full routing, sarvam-1 dropped
Transcribed text now flows through the exact same classify_skill → route_to_agent path a typed message takes - full access to everything. The reply-language step (getting a Tamil answer back) moved to humanize()'s new language parameter, running on qwen3:8b-16k - already chat-tuned, already multilingual, no few-shot/stop-sequence workarounds needed. Sarvam-1's call site was deleted, not just disabled.
Where this leaves it: sarvam-1 is proven capable of one thing (raw Q&A completion, with the right prompt shape) but isn't currently the right tool for either job in this pipeline - transcription isn't its job at all, and answering-with-full-context is qwen3's job here, already working. It stays pulled locally if a real use case for it shows up later - nothing about removing it deleted the model itself, just its one call site.
