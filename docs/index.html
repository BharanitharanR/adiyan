The problem

You can't scale being personally reachable.

Your best clients don't only need you during the scheduled hour — they need you the night before the hard conversation, the morning of the big decision. But you only have so many hours in a week, and every unscheduled text either interrupts your life or goes unanswered. Coaching that only exists inside a session leaves the other six days uncovered — and there's no way to be personally on-call for a full client roster.

The best advice is worth very little if it arrives a week too late.

The idea

So we build your twin, and put it where the conversation already happens.

Adiyan learns your coaching style — your frameworks, your tone, the way you actually talk to a client — and answers on your behalf through WhatsApp, the app already open on their phone. Your client isn't messaging a third-party bot with its own brand; as far as they can tell, they're messaging you.

You're in control

Nobody's added without asking. Nobody's stuck once they're in.

i.

Opt-in only, and you decide who's eligible. Your digital twin only replies to clients who've explicitly registered — everyone else is quietly ignored, not stored, not escalated.

ii.

Leaving is just as simple. A client sends "Unregister me" and every future message stops reaching your twin — no form, no support ticket, and you can remove anyone yourself at any time.

iii.

One account, and it's the one they already have. No separate password or profile for your client to manage — WhatsApp itself is the whole login.

Where it runs

Nothing leaves the building. Not even to us.

Client confidentiality isn't a nice-to-have in coaching — it's the job. The conversations your clients will have with their digital twin are often the ones they'd never put in writing anywhere else. Adiyan is built to run entirely on infrastructure you control — installed locally, not rented from someone else's cloud.

i.

The AI itself runs locally. No conversation is sent to an outside AI provider over the internet — the model that writes every reply lives on your own machine.

ii.

Conversation memory stays put. It's stored on that same machine so your twin can follow context — never mirrored to a third-party database.

iii.

No vendor privacy policy to take on faith. There's no outside party holding your clients' data in the first place — one less thing on your list of things to worry about.

It remembers

Your client doesn't have to re-explain themselves every time.

Your digital twin keeps a light thread of recent conversations, so picking up mid-thought doesn't mean starting from zero. It's always the same coach they left off with — not a new one every time they open the chat. And when they're back in the room with you, you're not starting from zero either.
