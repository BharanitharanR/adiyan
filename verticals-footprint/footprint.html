06 / 06 — TRACTION POST

Reddit

Reddit punishes anything that reads like marketing copy. Post as yourself, first-person, inviting real feedback — not the polished copy used elsewhere in this kit. Pick ONE subreddit to start, read its self-promotion rules first, and don't cross-post the identical text everywhere at once.

Subreddits worth trying (check each one's self-promo rule before posting)
  • r/SideProject — most tolerant of "I built this" posts
  • r/IndieHackers — wants the story and the numbers, not just the pitch
  • r/SmallBusiness — only if framed as "here's a tool," not a pitch to business owners as customers
  • r/artificial — leads with the technical angle (local install, plain-English config), not the business pitch
Suggested title
I built an AI WhatsApp assistant that installs on your own machine instead of a hosted subscription — looking for feedback
Suggested post body
Every "AI for your business" WhatsApp tool I looked at was a hosted subscription with a template chatbot underneath — you don't own the number, you don't own the rules, and you're paying monthly for someone else's server. I built Verticals instead: it installs on your own machine, connects to your own WhatsApp number, and you describe your business and your rules in plain English. No code. The part I actually care about is what happens underneath the conversation — it also orchestrates the flow you set up and analyses the business itself (there's a concrete example on the site: two competing restaurants, one's foot traffic spikes in a window the other's doesn't, and it suggests a specific move to test). There's a live demo on the site if anyone wants to try it themselves — type a one-line description of any business and it registers a real, working assistant in under a minute: https://verticals.co.in/ Genuinely want feedback, especially anything that seems broken or oversold.
